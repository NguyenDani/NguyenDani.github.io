About

I started in security as an intelligence analyst in the Texas Army National Guard, turning multi-source intelligence into threat assessments for leadership. That habit of asking what is actually a risk, and to whom, carried over when I moved into engineering with a computer science degree from the University of Houston.

Since then I've deployed and automated vulnerability scanning with Tenable Security Center and Nessus, hardened systems and Kubernetes clusters against DISA STIG and CIS baselines, and written policy as code so the next cluster starts out compliant. I prefer automating a control once over checking it by hand every week.

I now work in application security, embedding static and dynamic testing, secure code review, and penetration testing into the software development lifecycle. Outside of work I hold the eCPPT and eJPT, am working toward the OSCP, and spend time on HackTheBox, TryHackMe, and my own labs.
